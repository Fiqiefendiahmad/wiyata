# WIYATA Brand and Visual Identity

## Brand name
WIYATA

## Primary tagline
Your Database Learning Center

## Supporting statement
Learn, build, explore, and master database technologies — all in one place.

## Brand meaning
The name is inspired by Indonesian/Javanese cultural vocabulary associated with education and learning. The product name carries the cultural identity; the interface remains available in English and Bahasa Indonesia.

## App icon
See `../assets/wiyata-app-icon.png`.

The generated concept combines:
- A graduation cap: education and mastery
- A database cylinder: database technology
- An open book: learning and reference
- Gold ornament: subtle Indonesian-inspired identity
- Deep navy: professional technology aesthetic

## Color direction

Suggested starting palette:
- Deep navy: `#071A3A`
- Navy surface: `#10264A`
- Gold accent: `#F2B84B`
- Warm white: `#F7F8FA`
- Text primary: `#EAF0FA` in dark theme
- Neutral gray: `#8A96A8`
- Success: `#249B67`
- Warning: `#D99A32`
- Danger: `#D65353`

Check contrast in actual components before release.

## Typography

- UI: Inter or a comparable highly legible sans-serif.
- Code: JetBrains Mono or equivalent.
- Use clear hierarchy and avoid overly decorative type.

## Logo usage

- Use icon-only for app favicon and compact navigation.
- Use wordmark plus tagline for landing page.
- Do not stretch or recolor the supplied PNG.
- Create vector master artwork before final commercial branding.
- Verify trademark and domain availability before public launch.

## Design principles

- Professional developer tooling first.
- Heritage is subtle, not ornamental everywhere.
- Clear hierarchy and generous spacing.
- Dark and light themes.
- Dense workspace UI where useful; comfortable reading layouts in Knowledge Center.
