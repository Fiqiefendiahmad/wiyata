## Article template

Every article should include:
1. Title and summary
2. Learning objectives
3. Applicable database engine/version
4. Level: beginner/intermediate/advanced/expert
5. Explanation
6. Architecture diagram where useful
7. Syntax/code examples
8. Real-world use cases
9. Advantages, limitations, and trade-offs
10. Common mistakes
11. Troubleshooting
12. Practical exercise
13. Quiz or knowledge check where appropriate
14. Related articles
15. Official references
16. Author/reviewer
17. Published and last-reviewed dates
18. Language and translation status

## Source policy

- Prefer official vendor documentation for product-specific behavior.
- Cite version-specific official manuals.
- Use original explanations rather than copying vendor documentation.
- Respect licenses, trademarks, and copyright.
- Label community advice as community advice.
- Distinguish verified behavior from recommendations.
- Do not invent features, commands, licensing terms, or version support.
- Revalidate time-sensitive information such as supported versions and licensing.

## Editorial workflow

`draft -> technical review -> language review -> approved -> published -> review due`

High-risk operational instructions require technical review and warnings.

## AI knowledge assistant

- Retrieval-augmented answers over published articles and approved sources.
- Cite the article/source used.
- Ask or infer database engine/version only when clear; otherwise state assumptions.
- Separate vendor-documented facts from recommendations.
- Do not invent official references.
- Never suggest running destructive commands without explicit warnings and review.
